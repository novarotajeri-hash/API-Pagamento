About the tour

Late-afternoon tour to end the day watching the Jericoacoara sunset — one of the most famous in Brazil. A ride through the dunes and lagoons to the best spot to watch the sun set on the horizon.

Itinerary

  1. Sunset Dune
  2. A lagoon for a swim at dusk — depending on the time of year it may be lower; between June and September you're more likely to find it full
  3. The best viewpoint to watch the sunset

Tips & suggestions

  • Bring sunscreen, water and something to protect your phone from sand and water
  • Wear a swimsuit and footwear that can get wet
  • Times and the order of stops may vary depending on the tide and weather
  • Late-afternoon departure; the time changes with the season
  • Bring a light jacket for the way back and a charged camera
