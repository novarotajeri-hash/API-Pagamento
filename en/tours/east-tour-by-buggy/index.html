About the tour

Buggy tour on the eastern side of Jericoacoara with a vehicle just for your group (up to 4 people). The idea here is total freedom: you build the itinerary, decide how long to stay at each spot and choose which stops to make — no rush and no depending on a big group's pace.

Itinerary

  1. Pedra Furada — optional stop. If you want to include it, it's best done early in the morning to avoid the trail in strong sun. It's about a 1 km walk and, with the stop, adds at least 1h30 to the tour — time that is redistributed (reduced) among the other stops
  2. Árvore da Preguiça (Sloth Tree)
  3. Preá Beach
  4. Buraco Azul and Lagun Beach beach clubs — optional: choose both, just one or none
  5. Lagoa do Paraíso — you choose your preferred beach bar; the guide explains what each is like and helps you decide which suits your day best
  6. Return to Jericoacoara

Tips & suggestions

  • Bring sunscreen, water and something to protect your phone from sand and water
  • Wear a swimsuit and footwear that can get wet
  • Times and the order of stops may vary depending on the tide and weather
  • The itinerary is flexible: the beach club and Pedra Furada stops are optional — include them, pick just one or skip them.
  • The more stops you include, the less time at each. Since the buggy is just for your group, you set that balance with the guide.

You choose the beach bar — and how much to spend

  • Since the vehicle is just for your group, you decide which beach clubs to enter (or just check out) and how long to stay at each.
  • At Lagoa do Paraíso the beach bar is your choice: from the simplest and cheapest, to fine-dining options that don't even charge entry (like B&B), up to the trendiest — such as Alchymist (a busy bar with a higher minimum spend) or Caraúba (entry around R$50).
  • In short: you plan the day your way and have full control over how much you spend.
