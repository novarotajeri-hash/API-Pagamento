About the tour

Shared transport from Jeri to Nova Esperança, at Lagoa do Paraíso, returning in the late afternoon. It is the simplest beach hut on the lagoon, with no entry fee and moderate prices, for enjoying the crystal-clear water without spending much. We pick you up at your guesthouse in the morning, drop you at Nova Esperança and come back for you in the afternoon. Price is per person.

Itinerary

  1. Pickup at your guesthouse in Jeri, between 9:00 and 9:30 am
  2. Arrival at Nova Esperança, at Lagoa do Paraíso
  3. Free day to enjoy at your own pace
  4. Return between 3:30 and 4:00 pm, with drop-off at your guesthouse

Tips & suggestions

  • No entry fee
  • Bring sunscreen and swimwear
  • Food and drinks are not included

Cancellation & rain
Free cancellation up to 24 hours before, with a full refund. Within 24 hours or in case of no-show, there is no refund. If the tour cannot run because of heavy rain or safety reasons, you choose: reschedule at no cost or get your money back. Quick showers are common and usually do not cancel the tour.
Tourism fee
To enter Jericoacoara you pay the Sustainable Tourism Fee (TTS): R$41.50 per person, valid for up to 10 days. You can pay online on the city website or in person in Jijoca or at the village entrance. Paying ahead avoids queues on holidays and in high season. Exemptions set by the city (such as children up to 12, people over 60 and people with disabilities) require an ID document.
