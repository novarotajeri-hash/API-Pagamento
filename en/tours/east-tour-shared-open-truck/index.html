About the tour

Group tour on the 'jardineira' (an adapted open truck) exploring the eastern side of Jericoacoara. A relaxed itinerary through beaches, lagoons and beach clubs, with stops for swimming, resting and photos throughout the day.

Itinerary

  1. Árvore da Preguiça / Sloth Tree (first stop)
  2. Preá Beach
  3. Beach clubs: Buraco Azul and Lagun Beach
  4. Lagoa do Paraíso
  5. Return to Jericoacoara

Tips & suggestions

  • Bring sunscreen, water and something to protect your phone from sand and water
  • Wear a swimsuit and footwear that can get wet
  • Times and the order of stops may vary depending on the tide and weather

Entry fees and beach bars: worth considering

  • The beach club stops are part of the itinerary, and each one's entry is paid separately, on site — about R$40 per person on average.
  • At Lagoa do Paraíso the group stops at a pre-set beach bar, which may be one of the trendiest (like Alchymist), with a higher minimum spend or entry.
  • It's worth adding these entry fees to the tour price when planning. If you'd rather choose each beach bar and control that spending, the buggy and private options give you that freedom.
