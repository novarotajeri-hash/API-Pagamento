About the tour

Private tour on the eastern side of Jericoacoara, with a vehicle just for your group (up to 11 people). The itinerary is flexible: you choose the pace, the time at each stop and can arrange adjustments directly with the guide.

Itinerary

  1. Customizable itinerary arranged with the guide
  2. Pedra Furada — optional stop. If you want to include it, it's best done early in the morning to avoid the trail in strong sun. It's about a 1 km walk and, with the stop, adds at least 1h30 to the tour — time that is redistributed (reduced) among the other stops
  3. Árvore da Preguiça (Sloth Tree)
  4. Preá Beach
  5. Buraco Azul and Lagun Beach beach clubs — optional: choose both, just one or none
  6. Lagoa do Paraíso — you choose your preferred beach bar; the guide explains what each is like and helps you decide which suits your day best
  7. Return to Jericoacoara

Tips & suggestions

  • Bring sunscreen, water and something to protect your phone from sand and water
  • Wear a swimsuit and footwear that can get wet
  • Times and the order of stops may vary depending on the tide and weather
  • Since it's private, you can leave at whatever time you prefer
  • Let us know in advance if there are children, elderly people or any special needs

You choose the beach bar — and how much to spend

  • Since the vehicle is just for your group, you decide which beach clubs to enter (or just check out) and how long to stay at each.
  • At Lagoa do Paraíso the beach bar is your choice: from the simplest and cheapest, to fine-dining options that don't even charge entry (like B&B), up to the trendiest — such as Alchymist (a busy bar with a higher minimum spend) or Caraúba (entry around R$50).
  • In short: you plan the day your way and have full control over how much you spend.

Cancellation & rain
Free cancellation up to 24 hours before, with a full refund. Within 24 hours or in case of no-show, there is no refund. If the tour cannot run because of heavy rain or safety reasons, you choose: reschedule at no cost or get your money back. Quick showers are common and usually do not cancel the tour.
Tourism fee
To enter Jericoacoara you pay the Sustainable Tourism Fee (TTS): R$41.50 per person, valid for up to 10 days. You can pay online on the city website or in person in Jijoca or at the village entrance. Paying ahead avoids queues on holidays and in high season. Exemptions set by the city (such as children up to 12, people over 60 and people with disabilities) require an ID document.
