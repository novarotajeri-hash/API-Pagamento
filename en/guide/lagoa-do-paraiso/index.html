Lagoa do Paraíso beach club guide

Lagoa do Paraíso is in Jijoca de Jericoacoara, about 20 km from Jeri village. It is a freshwater lagoon, blue-green and famous for its hammocks in the water.

Its shore is long and has a bit of everything: lively beach clubs, fine-dining restaurants, simple beach huts, almost empty corners and even a public beach. The spot you choose shapes your day, so it pays to know what to expect from each one.

🏖 Public beach: Boqueirão

Boqueirão is the public beach of Lagoa do Paraíso, free to access. Since it has no facilities, it works best as a quick stop for a swim and photos rather than a place to spend the day.

Which one, in one line

  • Buzz and photos: Alchymist
  • Great food: Casa B&B
  • Comfort for less: Caraúba
  • Peace and quiet: Restaurante do Paulo or Nômades
  • Quick free stop: Boqueirão public beach

Best time to go

July is when the lagoon is fullest. From September to December the water is at its clearest.

This is also a windy season: the wind makes small waves, even on a lagoon, but it does not get in the way of swimming at all. On sunny days the water looks even bluer.

Tourism fee

To enter Jericoacoara you pay the Sustainable Tourism Fee (TTS): R$41.50 per person, valid for up to 10 days. You can pay online on the city website or in person in Jijoca or at the village entrance. Paying ahead avoids queues on holidays and in high season. Exemptions set by the city (such as children up to 12, people over 60 and people with disabilities) require an ID document.
