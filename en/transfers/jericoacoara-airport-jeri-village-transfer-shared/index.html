About the transfer

Shared transfer from Jericoacoara airport to Jeri village. You pay per person and share the vehicle with other travelers on a ride of about 1 hour — the most affordable way to reach Jeri.

How it works

  1. Pickup at Jericoacoara airport
  2. About 1 hour with other travelers
  3. Arrival in Jeri village
